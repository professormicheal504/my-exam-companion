JAMB 2027: Everything You Must Do Before You Register for UTME

MyExam Companion · September 21, 2026 · 8 min read

Every year, thousands of Nigerian students wake up on JAMB registration day to find they are missing something — a document, a code, a step nobody told them about. Then panic sets in. The truth is, JAMB 2027 will not wait for you to figure things out. The registration portal opens, fills up, and closes. If you are not prepared, you are spending another year at home. This guide breaks down every single thing you must have, do, and confirm before you even touch the JAMB registration portal.

Why Preparation Matters More Than You Think

JAMB registration is not just clicking a few buttons online. It is a multi-step process that requires documents, biometric data, correct personal information, and money — all aligned perfectly. One wrong detail, like a misspelled name or a missing NIN, can invalidate your registration entirely or cause issues at the exam centre.

JAMB has been strict about this in recent years. Candidates who had name mismatches between their NIN and their registration details were barred from writing the exam. You do not want to be that person.

⭐ Key Takeaway
The earlier you start these steps, the better. Most delays come from document corrections that take weeks — not the registration itself.

Step 1: Get Your National Identification Number (NIN)

This is the single most important requirement. JAMB will not register you without a valid NIN. Your NIN is issued by the National Identity Management Commission (NIMC) and ties your identity to your registration permanently.

How to get your NIN

  • Visit any NIMC office near you
  • Bring your birth certificate or age declaration, a passport photograph, and any existing ID
  • NIN enrolment is free
  • After enrolment, your NIN slip will be given to you or sent via SMS to your registered number
💡 Pro Tip
Do this at least four to six weeks before JAMB registration opens. NIMC offices get extremely busy once candidates realise they need it. Going early saves you hours of queuing — and potential stress.

Step 2: Confirm Your NIN Details Match Your Name Everywhere

This is the step most candidates skip — and the one that causes the most problems. Your NIN details (full name, date of birth, state of origin) must match exactly what you will enter on your JAMB form. Any mismatch — even a single letter difference like "Mohammed" vs "Muhammad" — will cause a verification failure.

Documents that must match your NIN

  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • School leaving certificate (WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB result)
  • O'Level result
⚠️ Warning
If there is any discrepancy between your NIN and your documents, visit NIMC first to correct your NIN record before registration begins. Do not wait until registration day.

Step 3: Decide Your Course and Institution

JAMB will ask you to choose your first choice institution, your course of study, and your second through fourth choice institutions. Do not guess these on registration day — research properly before you register.

How to choose wisely

  • Visit the JAMB brochure (available on the JAMB portal) to see which courses each institution offers and their exact subject requirements
  • Check the admission cutoff marks for your preferred institution from previous years
  • Confirm your O'Level subjects match what the course requires
💡 Pro Tip
Pick a first choice institution where your course is strong and your score is likely to qualify. Set your second choice as a backup in a less competitive institution. Many students ignore this and end up with no admission offer — even with a good score.

Step 4: Choose the Right Subject Combination

This is where a lot of candidates make costly mistakes. Your four UTME subjects must match your intended course. English Language is compulsory for every candidate. The other three subjects depend entirely on your course.

If you pick the wrong subjects, you can score 280 and still not get admission because your subjects do not qualify you for the course. Always verify in the JAMB brochure.

Step 5: Get a Valid Email Address and Phone Number

JAMB requires a personal, functional email address during registration. Your exam details, results, and admission letters will all go to this email. One email per candidate. One phone number per candidate.

💡 Pro Tip
Create a dedicated email address just for your JAMB and post-JAMB processes. Label it clearly and save the password somewhere safe. This one inbox will handle everything from CAPS to your admission confirmation letter.

Step 6: Get Your O'Level Results Ready

You will need to upload your O'Level results (WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB) during registration. Have your exam number and registration details handy. Make sure your results are accessible and not from a sitting that has verification issues.

If you are awaiting results, JAMB allows "awaiting result" registration — but you must upload the result before checking your admission status on CAPS.

Step 7: Prepare Your Passport Photograph

Your JAMB passport photograph must meet these requirements:

  • Taken against a white background
  • Recent — within the last three months
  • Full face clearly visible — no head coverings except for religious reasons
  • JPEG format, not more than 50KB, minimum 150×150 pixels
⭐ Key Takeaway
Blurry or incorrectly formatted photos are rejected by the portal and cause delays. Print extras and keep digital copies ready on your phone and email.

Step 8: Prepare Your Registration Fees

The JAMB registration fee has been set at ₦3,500 in recent years (confirm the exact amount on the official JAMB portal when registration opens). Additional fees include the purchase of your reading text.

⚠️ Warning
Never pay through third parties, WhatsApp agents, or unofficial websites. Fraud is rampant during JAMB season. Only pay through accredited JAMB CBT centres, official banks, or the JAMB e-Facility portal.

Step 9: Locate an Accredited JAMB CBT Centre Near You

JAMB registration is done at accredited Computer-Based Test (CBT) centres. Find one near you on the JAMB portal before registration opens.

  • Not all centres are accredited every year — verify before you go
  • Visit the centre beforehand so you know exactly where it is and how long it takes to get there
  • Go early on registration day — centres close when daily capacity is reached

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I register for JAMB without a NIN?

No. The NIN is mandatory for JAMB 2027 registration. JAMB uses it for identity verification and to prevent multiple registrations by the same candidate.

What if my name on my NIN is different from my school certificate?

You must correct the discrepancy before registration. Visit NIMC with supporting documents to update your NIN record, or contact your school to correct the certificate — depending on which document has the error.

Can I change my course or institution after JAMB registration?

Yes, JAMB allows a change of institution or course after the exam, but only within a specific window and for a fee. It is far better to choose correctly from the start to avoid unnecessary costs.

What age is required to write JAMB 2027?

Candidates must be at least 16 years old by the time of the examination. There is no maximum age limit.

Can I register for JAMB entirely from home?

You can start the process online on the JAMB e-Facility portal, but biometric capture — fingerprint and photograph — is done at an accredited CBT centre. You cannot complete registration entirely from home.

What happens if I miss the JAMB 2027 registration deadline?

You cannot write UTME that year. JAMB does not grant extensions for individual candidates. Monitor the official JAMB portal and their verified social media accounts for deadline announcements well in advance.

Your Pre-Registration Checklist

  • Valid NIN that matches all your documents
  • Course, institution and subject combination confirmed in the JAMB brochure
  • Personal email address and phone number ready
  • O'Level result accessible and ready for upload
  • Correctly formatted passport photograph (white background, JPEG, max 50KB)
  • Registration fees prepared — official channels only
  • Accredited CBT centre identified and visited in advance
Final word: JAMB 2027 registration will come and go quickly. The candidates who succeed are not the smartest — they are the ones who prepared earliest. Start every step on this list today, especially your NIN, and you will have nothing to worry about when the portal goes live.
