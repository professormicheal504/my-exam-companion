Common questions

Frequently asked

💳 What payment methods are accepted?

We currently accept bank transfers only via Paystack. Once your transfer is confirmed, your wallet is credited instantly.

⏳ Does my wallet balance expire?

No. Your wallet balance never expires. It stays in your account until you choose to spend it on exam access.

🔓 What does ₦500 unlock exactly?

₦500 unlocks the full Post-UTME past question pack for one university (e.g. UNIBEN), including all available years and all subjects in one payment.

🏛️ Which universities are available?

Currently UNIBEN and UNILAG. More universities are being added regularly. You can request one via the University Exam Hub.

🔄 Can I get a refund?

Wallet top-ups are non-refundable once credited. If you experience a payment issue, contact us at support@myexamcompanion.com.

🆓 Is JAMB practice really free?

Yes, completely free. JAMB CBT, WAEC, and NECO past questions are accessible to all users — no wallet or sign-in required.
