About Us

Welcome to My Exam Companion, your premier destination for world-class educational technology, comprehensive examination preparation, and cutting-edge e-learning resources. We are deeply committed to transforming the way students across Africa—and the globe—approach their academics.

Our Vision

We envision a world where every student, regardless of their geographical location or economic background, has access to the highest standard of educational materials. By leveraging modern technology, data analytics, and expertly curated content, we aim to completely eradicate the barriers to academic success.

Who We Are

My Exam Companion was founded by a passionate team of educators, software engineers, and data scientists who recognized a massive gap in the digital education sector. We noticed that while students had access to textbooks, they lacked an interactive, adaptive, and highly realistic testing environment that could adequately prepare them for modern Computer-Based Tests (CBT). Thus, My Exam Companion was born—a platform built by students, for students.

What We Do

  • State-of-the-Art CBT Simulation: Our flagship simulator flawlessly replicates the interface, timing mechanisms, and behavioral dynamics of actual standardized tests like JAMB (UTME), WAEC, NECO, and Post-UTME.
  • Massive Digital Library: We host a repository of tens of thousands of past questions, updated annually, ensuring our users are never caught off guard by changing examination patterns.
  • Granular Topic-Based Learning: We empower users to break down their study sessions into highly specific topics, allowing for targeted remediation of academic weaknesses.
  • In-Depth Explanations & Analytics: Every question is accompanied by a meticulously crafted, step-by-step explanation. Furthermore, our platform tracks user performance over time to provide actionable insights.

Our Core Values

Everything we build is centered around three core values: Accuracy, Accessibility, and Innovation. We are constantly iterating on our algorithms and content to ensure you get the best possible learning experience.
