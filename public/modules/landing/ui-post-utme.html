📋 What is UI Post UTME?

The University of Ibadan (UI) Post UTME is a supplementary screening examination conducted by the University of Ibadan, Nigeria's premier university. It is held annually for candidates who applied to UI through the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) and scored at or above UI's minimum cut-off mark.

The exam is conducted as a Computer-Based Test (CBT) and tests candidates in Use of English plus subjects relevant to their chosen course of study. Performance in this exam — combined with JAMB score and O'Level results — determines who gets admitted into Nigeria's first and most prestigious university.

  • Format: Computer-Based Test (CBT), Multiple Choice
  • Duration: Typically 60–90 minutes depending on faculty
  • Questions: 40–60 questions per sitting
  • Minimum JAMB Score to Participate: 200+ (varies by course; competitive courses require 250+)

🎯 UI Cut-Off Marks & Admission Score

UI uses a combined scoring system: your JAMB score + Post UTME score + O'Level results determine admission. The competition is extremely fierce as UI is Nigeria's premier university.

  • JAMB minimum (general): 200/400 to participate in screening
  • Medicine & Surgery: JAMB 270+, exceptional Post UTME score required (usually 80%+)
  • Law: JAMB 250+, very competitive Post UTME (75%+)
  • Engineering: JAMB 240+, high Post UTME score (70%+)
  • Social Sciences: JAMB 220-240+, competitive Post UTME
  • Arts & Education: JAMB 200-220+, moderate-to-high Post UTME score

Pro tip: UI admission is highly competitive. Even with a good JAMB score, an excellent Post UTME performance (70%+) is crucial. Many courses require aggregate scores of 70-80+ to secure admission.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the UI Post UTME cut-off mark? ▼
UI's Post UTME cut-off varies by faculty. Medicine requires 270+ in JAMB plus an exceptional Post UTME score (80%+). Law typically requires 250+ in JAMB with 75%+ Post UTME. Arts and Social Sciences courses have lower cut-offs around 200-220 in JAMB. Always check UI's official admission list for current year figures.
How many subjects are in UI Post UTME? ▼
UI Post UTME tests Use of English (compulsory) plus 2–3 other subjects relevant to your chosen course. Science courses include Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics or Biology. Arts courses include Literature, Government, History or Economics.
Is UI Post UTME computer-based (CBT)? ▼
Yes. University of Ibadan conducts its Post UTME screening as a Computer-Based Test (CBT). Candidates answer multiple-choice questions on a computer within a strict time limit. Practising on a CBT simulator like ours is essential for success.
Can I use UI past questions to prepare? ▼
Absolutely — it's the most effective strategy. Research shows that 70-80% of Post UTME questions are repeated or adapted from previous years. Practising past questions helps you understand question patterns, reduces exam anxiety, and improves time management significantly.
When is UI Post UTME 2024/2025? ▼
UI Post UTME dates are announced after JAMB results are released, typically between July and September. Check UI's official website (ui.edu.ng) or the JAMB portal for the exact schedule and registration details.
What makes UI Post UTME different from other universities? ▼
UI is Nigeria's premier and oldest university, making its Post UTME extremely competitive. The questions are more challenging and require deeper understanding. The cut-off marks are higher, and admission slots are limited. Excellence in both JAMB and Post UTME is essential.
