📋 What is Southern Delta University (SDU)?

The Southern Delta University (SDU) Post-UTME screening examination is a mandatory academic assessment conducted by SDU for UTME and Direct Entry candidates seeking undergraduate admission. Designed to evaluate the academic readiness of prospective students, this competitive entrance exam tests knowledge across core secondary school subjects aligned with chosen degree courses. Securing a high score in this screening is vital for candidates aiming to gain admission into high-demand faculties like Law, Engineering, and Health Sciences.

Established to serve the growing higher education demands of the Niger Delta region and Nigeria at large, the SDU screening exam has built a reputation for maintaining strict academic integrity and transparent merit-based admissions.

📚 Exam Format & Subject Structure

The SDU Post-UTME screening utilizes a streamlined computer-based test model to ensure speed, evaluation fairness, and transparency.

  • Format: Computer-Based Test (CBT)
  • Duration: 60 minutes
  • Number of Questions: 50 multiple-choice questions
  • Subjects Tested: Use of English plus three relevant subjects based on your chosen JAMB combination (e.g., Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry for Engineering).
  • Scoring: Graded out of 100%, which is subsequently combined with your UTME score to calculate an aggregate admission score.

🎯 Scoring & Pass Mark

To qualify for the SDU Post-UTME screening, candidates must attain the university's baseline JAMB cut-off mark, which typically ranges from 140 to 180 depending on the academic session. For the Post-UTME test itself, SDU requires a minimum pass mark of 50%. Highly competitive programs such as Medicine, Nursing, Law, and Computer Science usually demand an aggregate admission score of 65% and above, whereas less competitive courses in Arts and Social Sciences may accept aggregate scores starting from 50%.

🏆 Expert Tips to Pass Southern Delta University (SDU)

  1. Master the official SDU Post-UTME syllabus and systematically revise the four core subjects corresponding to your JAMB subject combination.
  2. Practice heavily with authentic SDU past questions under timed conditions to improve speed and familiarize yourself with recurring question structures.
  3. Hone your Computer-Based Test (CBT) navigation skills using practice software to build confidence and avoid technical delays on exam day.
  4. Prioritize the Use of English section, as grammar, comprehension, and vocabulary carry significant weight across all departmental paper combinations.
  5. Arrive at the designated campus CBT center early with all required documents, including your printed SDU screening slip, JAMB result slip, and valid identification.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When is Southern Delta University (SDU) conducted?
The SDU Post-UTME screening is typically conducted between August and October, shortly after the national UTME results are officially released by JAMB.

Q: How do I register for Southern Delta University (SDU)?
Candidates can register by visiting the official SDU admissions portal, generating a payment invoice for the screening fee via Remita, and completing the online application form with their JAMB details and O'Level results.

Q: Can I use past questions to prepare for Southern Delta University (SDU)?
Yes, practicing with authentic past questions is highly effective because SDU frequently repeats question patterns and core concepts from previous years. It also helps you master the time management necessary to answer 50 questions in 60 minutes.
