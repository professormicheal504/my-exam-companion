📋 What is Post UTME (UNIBEN)?

The University of Benin (UNIBEN) Post UTME is a mandatory Computer-Based Screening Examination conducted by UNIBEN for candidates seeking undergraduate admission into the institution. Candidates who score the minimum prescribed JAMB UTME cut-off mark (typically 200 and above) and select UNIBEN as their first choice are eligible to participate. Securing a high score in this screening is crucial, as final admission is strictly merit-based and determined by an aggregate score combining JAMB and Post UTME performance.

As one of Nigeria's premier first-generation tertiary institution assessments, the UNIBEN Post UTME is renowned across West Africa for its competitive nature and rigorous testing standards.

📚 Exam Format & Subject Structure

Understanding the structural layout of the UNIBEN Post UTME is vital for effective preparation and strategic time management during the test.

  • Format: Computer-Based Test (CBT) administered on campus at the Ugbowo Main Campus ICT Centre.
  • Duration: 30 minutes.
  • Number of Questions: 60 multiple-choice questions.
  • Subjects Tested: Use of English, Mathematics, General Paper (Current Affairs/UNIBEN history), and core subjects relevant to the candidate's prospective course (e.g., Biology, Chemistry, Physics for Medicine; Economics, Government, Literature for Arts/Social Sciences).
  • Scoring: Evaluated on a scale of 100 marks, which is subsequently combined with the candidate's JAMB score to determine the final aggregate percentage.

🎯 Scoring & Pass Mark

UNIBEN utilizes a strict 50:50 aggregate scoring model, where the candidate's JAMB score contributes 50% and the Post UTME score contributes 50% to the overall score. To qualify for general consideration, candidates must achieve a minimum benchmark pass mark of 50% (50 out of 100) in the screening exam. However, highly competitive courses such as Medicine and Surgery, Pharmacy, Law, Nursing, and Engineering typically require aggregate scores ranging between 65% and 75% for merit admission.

🏆 Expert Tips to Pass Post UTME (UNIBEN)

  1. Master UNIBEN Past Questions: Practice at least ten years of official UNIBEN CBT past questions, as the university frequently repeats question formats and concepts.
  2. Build Speed and Accuracy: Train with a strict 30-minute timer for 60 questions to get comfortable with answering each question in 30 seconds.
  3. Study General Knowledge: Review Nigerian current affairs, historical landmarks, and basic UNIBEN facts alongside your primary subjects.
  4. Focus on High-Yield JAMB Topics: Revisit key topics outlined in the JAMB syllabus, as UNIBEN aligns its questions with foundational secondary school curricula.
  5. Plan Exam-Day Logistics Early: Locate the Ugbowo ICT Centre early and arrive ahead of time to navigate biometric verification smoothly.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When is Post UTME (UNIBEN) conducted?
UNIBEN typically conducts its Post UTME screening between August and October, shortly after the release of the JAMB UTME results and the university's official call for applications.

Q: How do I register for Post UTME (UNIBEN)?
Eligible candidates register online through the official UNIBEN WAEUP Kofa portal (waeup.uniben.edu) by generating an RRR invoice, making payment, and completing the registration form.

Q: Can I use past questions to prepare for Post UTME (UNIBEN)?
Yes, practicing past questions is one of the most effective strategies because UNIBEN frequently repeats question patterns and test structures. It also conditions candidates to master the speed needed for the 30-minute exam window.
