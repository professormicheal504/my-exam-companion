The call for applications is now open for international students to apply for the University of Milan DSU Scholarship in Italy 2026/27. Studying and Living in Italy is very Cheap. The University of Milan Scholarships for International Students is available in All-Academic Fields and Majors and Open to all International Students. The scholarship offers a total tuition waiver paid in installments, and a grant of €6,000.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be an international student.
  • Must hold an equivalent degree for Master's or Bachelor's program.
  • Proof of English Language Proficiency.

The DSU Scholarship provides substantial financial support that completely waives the tuition fees and provides a yearly stipend, alongside free meals in the university canteens. Don't miss this opportunity to study in one of Europe's most beautiful and historic nations.
