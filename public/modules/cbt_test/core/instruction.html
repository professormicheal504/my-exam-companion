JAMB CBT Examination Instructions

🎯 Please Read All

This simulator gives you the same experience you will encounter in the actual JAMB CBT platform. For this test, you will be presented with 180 questions comprising:

  • 60 questions in English Language
  • 40 questions each from your three other selected subjects

How to Take the Test

  • Questions are presented one at a time, beginning with English Language.
  • Tap Next to proceed to the next question.
  • Tap Previous to return to a previous question.
  • Tap Submit after answering all questions to view your performance analysis and corrections.
  • If you wish to end the test before completion, tap Quit to cancel your progress.

Time Limit

You have a maximum of 120 minutes to complete the examination. If the time expires before you submit, your answers will be automatically submitted and your result summary will be displayed.
