How to Play

Live Chat
⚔️

Welcome to the Chat Arena

You are about to enter a fast-paced, live multiplayer quiz room. You'll compete against other students in real-time to answer questions as quickly as possible.

💬

Chat-Style Interface

Questions will be sent directly into the chat timeline by the Arena Bot.

⚡

Tap to Answer

Simply tap the option you want directly on the chat screen to select it, then confirm to lock it in.

⏱️

Beat the Clock

You have limited time for each question. The faster you answer correctly, the higher you rank on the leaderboard!
