JAMB Trivia Instructions

General Instructions

  • The Arena: You will see 4 balloons bouncing around the screen. Each contains one answer option.
  • The Objective: Read the question and click on the balloon containing the correct answer as fast as possible.
  • Time Control: Keep an eye on the timer! If you chose a bonus mode (e.g., 3|3), you will earn extra time for every question answered.

Scoring & Penalties

  • Correct Answer: The balloon pops, you earn points, and the next question loads immediately.
  • Incorrect Answer: You will lose points and a penalty will be applied! Be careful not to click wildly.
  • Last Two Penalty: If you use more than 2 attempts on a single question, you will earn NO points even if you eventually get it right.
