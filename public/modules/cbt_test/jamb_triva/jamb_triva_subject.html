💡 How Time Controls Work

JAMB Trivia challenges your speed and accuracy in a high-pressure environment.

  • 3 min: You have exactly 3 minutes to answer as many questions as possible.
  • 3 | 3: Start with 3 minutes, but gain a 3-second bonus for every question you answer!
  • Incorrect answers may penalize your score.
